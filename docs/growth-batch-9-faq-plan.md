## Proposed new FAQs (owner review before implementation)

### Do you replace leaking showers and waterproof shower enclosures?
Yes. I can assess leaking showers and carry out shower tray, enclosure and plumbing replacement work. Where a rebuild is needed, I can install suitable waterproof backing and tanking at vulnerable joints before fitting the new enclosure. The exact repair depends on the cause of the leak.

Placement: bathroom/shower service page; link to /advice/shower-replacement-waterproofing-rebuild and Merrow case study.

### Can you renovate a bathroom in Guildford?
I undertake bathroom plumbing and renovation work in Guildford and surrounding Surrey areas, including removal and replacement of baths, shower trays, toilets, basins and associated pipework. Send photos and a description for a quote.

Placement: bathroom page and Guildford page, avoiding duplication.

### Can you replace radiators and thermostatic radiator valves?
Yes. I replace radiators, towel radiators and thermostatic radiator valves (TRVs), and can discuss pipework adjustments where needed. Send radiator dimensions and photos so I can assess the job.

Placement: radiator service page.

### Can you investigate low water pressure or noisy pipes?
Yes. I can investigate plumbing-related pressure problems and water hammer, including issues at taps, valves and fittings. The cause needs checking on site before recommending a repair.

Placement: general plumbing / advice page.

### Do you take plumbing jobs in Wimbledon and South West London?
I am based in Guildford and consider plumbing enquiries from Wimbledon, Putney, Merton, Morden, Kingston and Richmond, subject to travel, job type and availability. Send the postcode and a description so I can confirm.

Placement: South West London coverage page, once relevant page is published.

### Can I call about an urgent leak outside normal working hours?
You can call to explain an urgent plumbing problem, including an active leak. Out-of-hours response and attendance depend on availability and location; a call does not guarantee immediate attendance.

Placement: urgent plumbing page.
