## Photo plan

Start with three to five strong, recent, consented images, then upload two
genuine job photographs per week for the first month and one or two per week
thereafter. A sustainable mix is:

- completed bathrooms and shower installations;
- toilets, taps, radiators and towel radiators;
- before/after pairs where the change is clear and honest;
- neat visible pipework and repair details;
- work in progress and Nigel working on genuine jobs;
- a clear logo/profile image and a strong completed-job cover image.

Do not photograph customer documents, house numbers, faces or possessions
without permission. Do not expose the private address of the service-area
business. Avoid stock, AI-generated or staged “job” photography. The aim is a
steady record of real work, not a one-off bulk upload.
