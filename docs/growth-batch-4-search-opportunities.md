## Page-level context

The homepage is the strongest organic URL: 2,759 impressions and 28 clicks in the main window. Google also tests both the homepage and `/plumber-guildford` for Guildford terms. Batch 4 therefore clarifies the hierarchy through titles, factual content and internal links rather than deleting, redirecting or multiplying pages.

High-impression pages with weak CTR include `/plumber-guildford` (1,366 impressions; 0.22% CTR), `/plumber-aldershot` (825; 0%), `/plumber-camberley` (582; 0.34%), and the homepage (2,759; 1.01%). The previous growth batches changed these pages recently, so Batch 4 makes focused snippet/entity improvements and leaves room for recrawling and re-ranking.

## Guardrails

- No new service/location URL is justified by this evidence.
- West Horsley is included as a nearby coverage fact, not as a new landing page.
- 24-hour wording promises availability to take calls and discuss available help, not guaranteed attendance or an arrival time.
- Nigel Harvey Plumbing remains the customer-facing identity; Nigel Harvey Ltd remains the legal name in structured data and footer/company contexts.
- Search performance should be reviewed after 30, 60 and 90 days before further page expansion.
