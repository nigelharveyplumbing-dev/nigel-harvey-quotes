Questions before you get in touch?

What plumbing jobs can I ask about?

Leaks, taps, toilets, bathrooms, showers, radiators, valves, pipework and other domestic plumbing work. Describe the job so Nigel can say whether it fits.

Can I call about a plumbing problem overnight?

Emergency callouts may be available overnight, but I may not answer while asleep. Please call or leave a message with your postcode and the problem. Response times and attendance are subject to availability.

Can I send photos of the problem?

Yes. You can add photos in WhatsApp after opening the conversation. Please do not send a video unless Nigel asks for one.

Do you work outside Guildford?

I consider jobs across Surrey and South West London, depending on the location and work involved. Send your postcode and job details so I can confirm whether I can help.

Your details enter Nigel’s enquiries list. He can review them and contact you using your preferred method to discuss the next step. Submission does not confirm a booking.
